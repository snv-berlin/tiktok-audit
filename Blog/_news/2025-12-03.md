New report on [Agentic AI Accounts](https://tiktok-audit.com/blog/2025/Agentic-AI-Accounts/): 354 AI-operated accounts amassed 4.5 billion views with 43,000+ posts on TikTok, yet TikTok labeled less than 1.4% as AI-generated. Accounts frequently contained content sexualizing female bodies, false news, and anti-immigrant narratives. Full report at [aiforensics.org](https://aiforensics.org/work/agentic-ai-accounts).
