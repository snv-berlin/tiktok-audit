We published a new report on [TikTok's election information labels during Poland's 2025 Presidential Election](https://tiktok-audit.com/blog/2025/TikTok-Polish-Elections/): labels were applied sporadically, the Polish diaspora was excluded from seeing them, and 80% of videos spreading election fraud allegations went unlabeled. Full report at [aiforensics.org](https://aiforensics.org/work/tiktok-polish-elections).
