Martin Degeling was interviewed for the Deutschlandfunk feature ["Doomscroll – Wie TikTok Jugendliche im Wahlkampf beeinflusst"](https://www.hoerspielundfeature.de/doomscroll-wie-tiktok-jugendliche-im-wahlkampf-beeinflusst-100.html) (in German), about how TikTok's algorithm shapes young users' political content and what that means for first-time voters. The feature accompanies two 16-year-olds in Mecklenburg-Vorpommern documenting how their feeds spiral towards one-sided political content during the state election campaign.
