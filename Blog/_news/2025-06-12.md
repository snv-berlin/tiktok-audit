New report on [TikTok's Research API](https://tiktok-audit.com/blog/2025/TikTok-Research-API-Problems/): one in eight videos — including official TikTok content and ads — is inaccessible through the API with no explanation, undermining independent research under the DSA. Published on [TechPolicy.Press](https://www.techpolicy.press/unpacking-tiktoks-data-access-illusion/) and [aiforensics.org](https://aiforensics.org/work/tk-api).
