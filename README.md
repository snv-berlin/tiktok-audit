# TikTok Audit
A collection of code, data and information related to our audit of TikTok. The blog is available at [tiktok-audit.com](https://tiktok-audit.com).

*Note: The SNV/interface project ended in 2024. The blog is now maintained by Martin Degeling, continuing the work through [AI Forensics](https://aiforensics.org).*

# Blog Posts and Reports

## 2025
* 2025-12-03 **[Prompt, Upload, Repeat: Agentic AI Accounts Flood TikTok](https://tiktok-audit.com/blog/2025/Agentic-AI-Accounts/)** — 354 AI-operated accounts amassed 4.5 billion views; TikTok labeled less than 1.4% as AI-generated. Full report: [aiforensics.org](https://aiforensics.org/work/agentic-ai-accounts).
* 2025-10-16 **[From FYP to WW3](https://tiktok-audit.com/blog/2025/From-FYP-to-WW3/)** — During the 2025 NATO Summit, TikTok's For You Pages prioritized military content and war speculation while search showed news coverage. Covered by [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/). Full report: [aiforensics.org](https://aiforensics.org/work/fyp-to-ww3).
* 2025-07-31 **[AI Generated Algorithmic Virality](https://tiktok-audit.com/blog/2025/GenAI-Algorithmic-Virality/)** — 25% of TikTok's top search results contain synthetic AI imagery; only half is labeled. Covered in DER SPIEGEL (39/2025). Full report: [aiforensics.org](https://aiforensics.org/work/gen-ai-slop).
* 2025-07-14 **[Towards Transparent Recommender Systems](https://tiktok-audit.com/blog/2025/Towards-Transparent-Recommender-Systems/)** — ISD dispatch on why current DSA transparency measures fall short for assessing political bias, and what researchers need. Full report: [isdglobal.org](https://www.isdglobal.org/digital-dispatch/towards-transparent-recommender-systems-lessons-from-tiktok-research-ahead-of-the-2025-german-federal-election/).
* 2025-06-12 **[TikTok's Research API: Problems without Explanations](https://tiktok-audit.com/blog/2025/TikTok-Research-API-Problems/)** — One in eight videos is inaccessible through TikTok's Research API with no explanation. Published on [TechPolicy.Press](https://www.techpolicy.press/unpacking-tiktoks-data-access-illusion/). Full report: [aiforensics.org](https://aiforensics.org/work/tk-api).
* 2025-06-09 **[TikTok's Polish Elections Labels: Only Sometimes, And Only For Some](https://tiktok-audit.com/blog/2025/TikTok-Polish-Elections/)** — TikTok excluded 20M+ diaspora users from election labels; 80% of videos spreading fraud allegations went unlabeled. Full report: [aiforensics.org](https://aiforensics.org/work/tiktok-polish-elections).
* 2025-03-24 **[Crushing Comments: Gendered Harassment During the 2024 EU Parliament Elections on TikTok](https://tiktok-audit.com/blog/2025/Crushing-Comments/)** — Female candidates faced substantially higher harassment in TikTok comments; objectification, misgendering, and competence double-standards documented across FR, DE, and HU. Full report: [isdglobal.org](https://www.isdglobal.org/publication/crushing-comments-gendered-harassment-during-the-2024-eu-parliament-elections-on-tiktok/).
* 2025-02-22 **[Wahlkampf im Feed: How TikTok Handles Party-Political Content Before the 2025 German Federal Election](https://tiktok-audit.com/blog/2025/Wahlkampf-im-Feed/)** — TikTok labeled only 59% of official party content as political; AfD accounted for 49% of first political videos shown across test accounts. Full report: [isdgermany.org](https://isdgermany.org/wahlkampf-im-feed-wie-tiktok-mit-parteipolitischen-inhalten-im-vorfeld-der-bundestagswahl-2025-umgeht/).
* 2025-02-07 **[TikTok's Ad Library: Persistent Issues and New Challenges](https://tiktok-audit.com/blog/2025/TikTok-Ad-Lib-Perstisting-Issues/)** — Interface and data problems that hinder research use of TikTok's DSA-mandated ad library.

## 2024
* 2024-11-20 **[TikTok's Effect on Health](https://tiktok-audit.com/blog/2024/TikToks-Effect-On-Health/)** — Survey and scraped data findings on how TikTok's content affects young adults' body image and health. Final report of the SNV RSBA series. (Anna Semenova, Martin Degeling, Greta Hess)
* 2024-11-19 **[Systemic Risk for Elections? TikTok's Algorithmic Amplification of Political Content](https://tiktok-audit.com/blog/2024/Systemisches-Risiko-Wahlen/)** — ISD Germany study on asymmetric amplification around Brandenburg's 2024 election; AfD content appeared in 9 of 10 test accounts. Full report: [isdgermany.org](https://isdgermany.org/systemisches-risiko-fuer-wahlen/).
* 2024-08-28 **[Understanding TikTok's For You Feed](https://tiktok-audit.com/blog/2024/For-You-Feed/)** — Analysis of the For You Page algorithm and content distribution patterns.
* 2024-07-15 **[Search Suggestions](https://tiktok-audit.com/blog/2024/Search-Suggestions/)** — How TikTok's search suggestions influenced content exposure in Germany before the EU elections. Covered by [Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/tiktok-german-voters-afd/) and [Netzpolitik](https://netzpolitik.org/2024/afd-in-tiktok-studie-mit-der-erregungsmaschine-in-den-kaninchenbau/).
* 2024-06-28 **[TikTok's Effect on Consumer Protection](https://tiktok-audit.com/blog/2024/TikToks-Effect-on-Consumer-Protection/)** — Summary findings from the consumer protection risk scenario.
* 2024-06-07 **[Consumer Protection on TikTok](https://tiktok-audit.com/blog/2024/Consumer-Protection-on-TikTok/)** / **[How Young Adults Use TikTok](https://tiktok-audit.com/blog/2024/How-Young-Adults-Use-TikTok/)** / **[Hidden Costs and Financial Strain](https://tiktok-audit.com/blog/2024/Hidden-Costs-and-Financial-Strain/)** — Three companion posts on the consumer protection RSBA scenario.
* 2024-04-25 **[Decoding Success: Temu, Shein, TikTok](https://tiktok-audit.com/blog/2024/Decoding-Success-Temu-Shein-Tiktok/)** — Analysis of e-commerce platform advertising strategies on TikTok.
* 2024-03-28 **[Hashtag Horizon](https://tiktok-audit.com/blog/2024/Hashtag-Horizon/)** — Hashtag distribution and trend analysis.
* 2024-03-01 **[RSBA Step 3: Data Collection](https://tiktok-audit.com/blog/2024/TikTok-RSBA-Step3/)** / **[Step 2: Scenario Design](https://tiktok-audit.com/blog/2024/TikTok-RSBA-Step2/)** / **[Step 1: Methodology](https://tiktok-audit.com/blog/2024/TikTok-RSBA-Step1/)** — The full risk-scenario-based audit methodology.
* 2024-02-09 **[DSA and TikTok](https://tiktok-audit.com/blog/2024/DSA-TikTok/)** — Assessment of TikTok's DSA compliance status.
* 2024-01-10 **[Temu and TikTok](https://tiktok-audit.com/blog/2024/temu-tiktok/)** — Comparative analysis of Temu's advertising strategy on TikTok.

# Earlier Updates
* 2023-07-07 We have published an extended [list of variables](https://github.com/snv-berlin/tiktok-audit/tree/main/Data%20Access) TikTok uses.
* 2023-06-02 We have also [submitted a comment](https://ec.europa.eu/info/law/better-regulation/have-your-say/initiatives/13626-Digital-Services-Act-conducting-independent-audits/F3424007_en) on the public consultation process of the European Commission on the draft delegated act on audits. A summary is available on our [website](https://www.stiftung-nv.de/en/publication/auditing-platforms-under-dsa-snvs-consultation-response-draft-delegated-act).
* 2023-05-23 Based on our experience with TikTok we've submitted [feedback](https://ec.europa.eu/info/law/better-regulation/have-your-say/initiatives/13817-Delegated-Regulation-on-data-access-provided-for-in-the-Digital-Services-Act/F3422376_de) to the European Commission's Call for Evidence for the Delegated Regulation on data access provided for in the Digital Services Act.
* 2023-04-19 Our analysis of the search function in the TikTok app has revealed the names of block lists TikTok is using. Our results are summarized in a [mastodon thread](https://chaos.social/@mrtn3000/110225624888667893).
* 2023-04-13 To show the [differences between the metadata](https://chaos.social/@mrtn3000/110191269705924454) available on the TikTok website and within the app, we've published [schemas](https://github.com/snv-berlin/tiktok-audit/commit/59ac5fb922a08c78ebef999a13d0443ba4a4dbad) of each video from the different sources.
* 2023-03-16 Since the summer of 2022, we have been monitoring the TikTok websites for the videos shown there. A graph-based representation of the most common hashtags is available [here](https://martin.degeling.com/snv/fyp-hashtags-by-week/). Code and data [here](https://github.com/snv-berlin/tiktok-audit/tree/main/TikTok%20Web/hashtag-combinations-by-week).
* 2023-02-07 We have published a paper on ["Auditing Recommender Systems"](https://www.stiftung-nv.de/en/node/3444) that proposes a risk-scenario-based audit process which will guide our TikTok analysis.
* 2022-12-05 Added the TikTok Algo 101 document and translations. The document was first discussed in the [New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2021/12/05/business/media/tiktok-algorithm.html) in December 2021, but was online in [Chinese](https://blog.csdn.net/m0_37586850/article/details/113488345) since January 2021.
